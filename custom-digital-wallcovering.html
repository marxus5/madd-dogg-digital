FAQ

Questions about custom digital wallcovering.

What is custom printed wallcovering?

Custom printed wallcovering is produced directly from digital artwork. It lets designers create bespoke wallpapers, murals and large-scale graphics in high resolution, from intricate patterns to full-wall images.

How do you make sure colors match my design?

We use G7 Master Printer color management, and our color matching step provides realistic samples and mock-ups for your approval before production begins.

What do you need to quote my project?

Send us your quantities, substrate and timeline through the quote form, along with your artwork and any performance requirements. We will follow up to discuss materials, samples and timing.

What are your minimum orders, fire ratings and lead times?

These depend on the material and project scope. Send us your quantities, substrate and timeline through the quote form and we will confirm details.
