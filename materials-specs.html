What Shapes Your Spec

Four things that decide minimums, fire ratings and lead times.

Substrate

The material you choose drives what is available and how it performs.

Application

Interior or exterior use, and the space it goes into.

Quantity

How much you need affects minimums and scheduling.

Timeline

When you need it determines how we plan production and delivery.

Quote Checklist

What to send us for a quote.

The more we know up front, the faster we can confirm materials, samples and timing.

  • The material or substrate you have in mind (or the look and performance you need)
  • Interior or exterior application
  • Quantities and sizes
  • Timeline and delivery location
  • Any fire rating or other performance requirements
  • Your artwork or design direction
FAQ

Questions about materials and specs.

What building materials can you print on?

Digital printing opens up customization on a variety of building materials, for interior and exterior applications. Tell us the material and application and we will confirm what is possible.

What are your minimum orders, fire ratings and lead times?

These depend on the material and project scope. Send us your quantities, substrate and timeline through the quote form and we will confirm details.

What should I send for a quote?

Send your quantities, substrate and timeline through the quote form, along with your artwork and any performance requirements such as fire rating.

Who do you work with?

We serve interior designers, architects and building material manufacturers.
