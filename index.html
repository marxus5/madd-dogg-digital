FAQ

Digital wallcovering and printing questions.

What is digitally printed wallcovering?

Digitally printed wallcovering is custom wallcovering produced directly from digital artwork. It lets designers create bespoke wallpapers, murals and large-scale graphics in high resolution, from intricate patterns to full-wall images.

Do you print custom acoustical panels?

Yes. We print custom acoustical sound boards that improve both the look and the acoustic performance of offices, studios and public spaces.

Who do you work with?

We serve interior designers, architects and building material manufacturers.

How do you make sure colors match my design?

We use G7 Master Printer color management, and our color matching step provides realistic samples and mock-ups for your approval before production begins.

What are your minimum orders, fire ratings and lead times?

These depend on the material and project scope. Send us your quantities, substrate and timeline through the quote form and we will confirm details.
